# Help with problems

The usual fixes. First thing to try for almost anything: make sure Mercify is up to date (it updates itself when you open it).

<details>
<summary><b>I can't see the panels in my game</b></summary>

- Set your game to **Windowed (Fullscreen)** or **Borderless** in its graphics settings. Mercify can't appear over true fullscreen.
- Check your game is listed under **Only show over these apps** in Settings. If not, see [Using Mercify with another game](GUIDE.md#other-games-and-monitors).
- Press `Ctrl` + `` ` ``: you may have hidden them.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>The panels say "Waiting for Spotify"</b></summary>

Click the message to open Settings.

- Anything not green? Press the big button.
- All green? Make sure Spotify is open. If it is, press **Redo** next to *Mercify extension*. This is usually needed after Spotify updates itself.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Windows says "Windows protected your PC"</b></summary>

Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Windows shows this for new programs it hasn't seen much of yet. It goes away as more people use Mercify.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>The overlay disappears after I click it, or my game stops taking keys</b></summary>

Right after it happens, right-click the Mercify tray icon, choose **Copy diagnostics**, and paste it into a message or an [issue](https://github.com/Kain-Mercer/Mercify/issues). It shows what Windows did with its windows, without your other window titles.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>I can't join a lobby ("Couldn't reach any lobby relay")</b></summary>

A firewall, or a school or work network, may be blocking the lobby servers (ports 8084, 8884 and 8081). Try another network, like your phone's hotspot, to check.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Clicking an album does nothing</b></summary>

Albums open in the **Playlist** panel, so turn that panel on in Edit Mode (`Ctrl` + `Shift` + `` ` ``). If Settings offers **Finish setup**, press it (Spotify restarts once).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>The panels have a black box behind them</b></summary>

Some graphics drivers do this. [Open an issue](https://github.com/Kain-Mercer/Mercify/issues) and we'll help you switch on a fix.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>A shortcut doesn't work</b></summary>

Another program is probably using it. Mercify tells you when it starts if a shortcut couldn't be set up. **Change** next to *Hotkeys* in Settings opens the settings file where they can be changed; restart Mercify afterwards.

</details>

---

**Still stuck?** [Open an issue](https://github.com/Kain-Mercer/Mercify/issues) and say what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.
