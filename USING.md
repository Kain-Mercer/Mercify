# Using Mercify

## The panels

You choose where each panel goes and how see-through it is.

- **Now Playing:** album art, the song, a seek bar, a volume slider, and play/pause, skip, shuffle, repeat and like buttons. Drag the seek bar or volume to where you want them, or scroll your mouse wheel over the volume. Long song names scroll by once when a new song starts, and again when you hover over them.
- **Search:** type a song, artist, playlist or album. Click a song to play it, or open a playlist or album to see its songs.
- **Playlist:** browse any of your playlists, your Liked Songs, or an album from search, and click a song to play it.
- **Lobby** and **Session Playlist:** for [listening with friends](LOBBIES.md).
- **Notifications:** messages like "Kain added a song" in a lobby.

## Shortcuts

| Press | To |
|---|---|
| `Ctrl` + `` ` `` | Show or hide the panels (`` ` `` is the key under `Esc`) |
| `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `` ` `` | Enter Edit Mode, to move and resize the panels |
| `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `Space` | Jump to search. Type, then `Enter` plays the top result, or `Esc` takes you back to your game |

These only work while you're in your game, so they don't get in the way of other programs. The Edit Mode shortcut works everywhere.

Clicking Mercify's buttons doesn't take the keyboard away from your game, so you can keep moving right after you skip a song. Only typing in a search box does, and `Enter`, `Esc` or picking a song gives it straight back.

## Arranging your panels (Edit Mode)

Press `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `` ` ``. It works much like World of Warcraft's own Edit Mode:

- **Drag** a panel to move it, or drag an edge or corner to resize it. Panels snap neatly to each other and the screen edges. Hold `Alt` while dragging to place them freely.
- **Arrow keys** nudge the selected panel one step at a time.
- **Click** a panel to:
  - turn it on or off,
  - set how see-through it is normally,
  - set how see-through it is when you hide the overlay (for example, keep Now Playing faintly visible while everything else disappears),
  - make it bigger or smaller.

Press `Esc` or **Done** when you're finished. Your layout saves by itself.

Notifications still pop up while the overlay is hidden. Set the Notifications panel's *Opacity when hidden* to 0% if you'd rather they didn't, or untick *Show this panel* to turn them off.

## Compact Now Playing

Hover over Now Playing and click the small arrows in its top-right corner to shrink it to a compact bar with the song and previous, play/pause and next. Click the arrows again to make it full size. It shrinks and grows in place, towards the nearest corner of your screen. You can also switch it with **Compact view** in Edit Mode, and move and resize the compact bar there like any other panel.

When you hide the overlay with `Ctrl` + `` ` ``, the compact bar stays on screen at its "hidden" see-through level and **still works**: pause or skip without bringing everything back. It brightens while your mouse is over it. To hide it completely too, set its *Opacity when hidden* to 0%.

## Using it with other games

Mercify shows up over World of Warcraft out of the box. To add another game:

1. Click the Mercify icon in the system tray (bottom right of the taskbar) to open **Settings**.
2. Under **Only show over these apps**, press **Pick the app in front**.
3. Within 5 seconds, click into your game. It's added to the list.

To have Mercify always visible, wherever you are, switch **Only show over these apps** off.

## More than one monitor

When you click over to something on another monitor (a browser, Discord), Mercify stays up over your game, as long as the game is still showing on its own screen. It hides if you minimise or close the game, or put another window in front of it on the same screen. Your shortcuts go back to normal in the other program until you click back into the game.

Untick **Stay up while I use another monitor** in Settings if you'd rather it hid. Settings also shows a line saying why Mercify is showing or hidden right now, handy if it isn't doing what you expect.

## Settings

Open **Settings** by clicking the tray icon. You can:

- start Mercify with Windows (it then waits quietly in the tray instead of opening Settings),
- open Spotify automatically when Mercify starts,
- choose whether Spotify's window is tucked into the tray when Mercify opens,
- choose which games Mercify shows over,
- start or join a [listening lobby](LOBBIES.md),
- see which version you have and check for [updates](UPDATES.md),
- re-run any setup step if something stops working.
